How much does it cost to ship a car?

Price depends on distance, vehicle size, trailer type, the season, and whether the vehicle runs. The quote form gives you an all-in number for your exact route.

How long does transport take?

Transit time grows with distance, and weather and traffic can shift it. You get an estimated delivery window when you book, and the driver calls ahead before arrival.

Is my vehicle insured during shipping?

Yes. Every carrier we assign carries cargo insurance, and the vehicle is inspected and documented at pickup and again at delivery.
